FAQ

Common Questions

How long does an ERP implementation typically take?

Implementation timelines depend on scope and complexity. A focused ERPNext deployment for a single company typically takes 8–14 weeks. Multi-company or heavily customised Oracle APEX projects can range from 3–9 months. We provide a detailed project schedule during discovery.

Do you offer post-launch support?

Yes. All projects include a 90-day hypercare period after go-live. Beyond that, we offer flexible SLA-backed support agreements covering system monitoring, bug fixes, enhancements, and user training — tailored to your operational requirements.

Can you integrate with our existing legacy systems?

Absolutely. Integration is a core capability. We have experience connecting modern ERP and analytics platforms to legacy systems (SAP, custom databases, in-house tools) via REST APIs, file-based integrations, direct DB connectors, and custom middleware — regardless of age or technology stack.

What is your pricing model?

We offer fixed-price project engagements for well-defined scopes, and time-and-materials for evolving or exploratory projects. Support agreements are priced as monthly retainers based on SLA tier. We provide transparent proposals with no hidden fees.
